## 🧠 About Me

I'm an **AI Engineer & Backend Engineer** at **Tata Consultancy Services**, where I design agentic RAG systems and production-grade microservices that process real enterprise workloads — think 100K+ invoices a month, 50,000+ policy records analyzed for drift in real time, and LLM-powered agents that reason, retrieve, and act with human-in-the-loop oversight.

I sit at the intersection of **Generative AI** and **distributed backend systems** — building the LangChain/LangGraph agent on one side and the Kafka-driven, Kubernetes-deployed infrastructure that keeps it reliable at scale on the other.

- 🔭 **Currently building:** Agentic RAG for health insurance, AI-powered ITSM triage & recommendation systems, and event-driven microservices modernizing claims/invoice processing
- 🌱 **Currently deepening:** Fine-tuning (LoRA/QLoRA), multi-agent orchestration, and inference optimization
- 🎯 **Known for:** Turning "top 5 out of 400+ teams" hackathon prototypes into production-viable systems that get COO-level recognition
- 🤝 **Open to:** Mentoring, technical talks on GenAI/Agentic AI, and backend architecture collaborations
